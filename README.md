# Make the Judge Smile

A single line about a small good thing that happened today, written for the make.fun competition "Make the Judge Smile". Running `python3 main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`: today the warung owner remembered I take my tea without sugar, three months after my last visit — and refused to let me pay for it. One concrete detail, one warm surprise, no stock images.
